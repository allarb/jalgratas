Spinning Forward: Inside Velonova's Electric Shift

April 5, 2025

"E-bikes aren’t just about adding motors — they’re about removing limits."

We caught up with Amira Chen, the innovative CEO of Velonova, during a product launch in Berlin. Known for her engineering background and love of mountain biking, Amira has led the company through a major pivot into the electric bike market.

The Pivot

“We started as a small commuter brand,” says Amira. “But riders kept asking for more — more range, more speed, more adventure. The electric revolution let us meet them where they are.”
