Bicycle Hall of Fame: 1974 Raleigh Chopper Mk2

Year: 1974
Manufacturer: Raleigh
Model: Chopper Mk2
Original Price: €55.00

A Two-Wheeled Icon

The Raleigh Chopper Mk2 became an instant cultural phenomenon in the 1970s. With its muscle car-inspired styling, banana seat, and high-rise handlebars, it captured the rebellious spirit of a generation.

"Designed to stand out on the street, the Chopper defined an era of bold design and youthful independence."

Technical Specifications

Frame Material High-Tensile Steel
Wheel Size 16" front / 20" rear
Brakes Front caliper, rear drum
Paint Flame Red with Chrome Accents
Saddle Banana-style with backrest

Cultural Relevance

  • Reflected 1970s youth freedom
  • Influenced by hot rod aesthetics
  • Banned in some countries for safety
  • Now a prized collector’s item

Unique Traits

• 3-speed gear shift on top tube
• Ape-hanger handlebars
• Extra padded seat
• Oversized rear tire
