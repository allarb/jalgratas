About Pedal Faster

Our Story

Est. 2020 | Reimagined 2025

From our first print issue in 2020 to today's multi-platform media network, Pedal Faster has pioneered the future of cycling journalism. With bureaus now in 8 countries, we bring global perspectives to the evolving world of smart, sustainable mobility.

Our 2025 Vision

In an era of transportation transformation, we champion cycling as the nexus of technology, sustainability and urban design. Our expanded Reality+ platform merges immersive digital experiences with hands-on product testing and policy analysis.
