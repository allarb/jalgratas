Cycle Lane Defence: Know Your Rights

Updated: March 2025

This article outlines cyclist protections and key legal points. Local regulations may vary—check with your regional authority.

Riding with Confidence

Whether commuting or riding recreationally, understanding your legal standing as a cyclist can help you stay safe and assert your space on the road.

"Bike lanes are not a courtesy — they’re a right. Blocking them is a traffic offence."

Protected Legal Rights

  • Right to uninterrupted use of marked cycle lanes
  • Right to take the center of a lane for visibility and safety
  • Right to report cycle lane obstructions
  • Legal protection in dooring and overtaking incidents
  • Right to file police reports for aggressive driving

Risk Areas to Watch

Common situations where cyclists' rights are violated include:

  • Delivery vans blocking bike lanes
  • Motorists turning across bike lanes without yielding
  • Roadworks redirecting cyclists into traffic
  • Lack of enforcement for bike lane infractions

Post-Incident Checklist

  1. Move to a safe area
  2. Take clear photos and video
  3. Record plate numbers
  4. File a police report
  5. Consult with legal or medical services
